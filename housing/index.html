Design

Strap pack

  • Stack: battery on the head side, ESP32 Lite on top on foam tape, low-profile button under the lid. The 8-pin connector pair and the coil splices sit in a channel beside the board, where the battery leaves room down to the floor.
  • Battery leads soldered to the board's battery socket pins: no plug sticking out, and the socket-type question goes away.
  • Lid snaps onto the base: a lip slides inside the base walls, four bumps click into windows. Three posts in the lid hold the board down.
  • USB-C is the only opening: a notch in the lid wall with a funnel so plug bodies seat fully. Silicone plug for snow.
  • Coil leaves through a strain-relief cone at the front; inside, a cradle holds it (add a drop of glue).
  • Strap clamp: the plate snaps under the base with four hooks and squeezes the strap in a ribbed channel. No threading, no screws.
  • Button: a TPU cap (soft touch, seals the hole) presses a 12×12×4.3 switch held in a pocket in the lid.

Screen pod + frame bar

  • Pod hangs inside the goggle at the top centre, screen facing the lens, tilted down (set the tilt above). Front holds the screen behind a chamfered window; the back snaps in with two bumps and presses the screen with four ribs.
  • Stem on the pod back goes up through the frame's top vent and clicks into the frame bar on top of the frame. Pod and bar clamp the frame rail between them: no glue, no screws.
  • Bar holds the IMU rigidly on the frame and takes the coil through a strain-relief cone. Six wires go down the stem's channel to the screen; SDA/SCL and power branch to the IMU inside the bar.
  • No vent at the top centre? Make a 16×5 mm slot, or glue the pod instead.

Assembly order

  1. Thread the coil through the pack's front cone, then splice it to the 8-pin male pigtail (heat-shrink).
  2. Solder the female pigtail, button wires (GPIO27, G), battery leads (to the battery socket's pins; cut and solder one wire at a time) and the 100 kΩ/100 kΩ divider (battery + → GPIO35 → G, lying flat on top of the board near the left pin row) to the ESP32 Lite.
  3. Pack base: battery on foam tape, Kapton over it, board on 2 mm foam tape, connector pair and splices into the channel, coil into the cradle with glue.
  4. Switch into the lid pocket (trim its legs to 1.5 mm), TPU cap in from outside; snap the lid on.
  5. Goggle end: thread the coil's other end through the bar's cone; solder 6 wires through the stem to the screen; bridge CS→GND and RES→VCC on the screen; IMU to SDA/SCL + power.
  6. Screen into the pod front, pod back clicks on. From inside the goggle push the stem up through the vent into the bar until it clicks; IMU into the bar, lid on.
  7. Strap plate: lay the strap in the channel, snap the plate under the pack.
