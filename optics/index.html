Ski HUD optics bench

The goggle HUD at real scale, in millimetres. Nothing sits in front of your eye: a small projector beside the inner corner of your right eye shines onto a film patch on the goggle lens, and the lens reflects the picture back to you. This is how Everysight, Livemap and REKKIE do it. Every ray is traced through the real surfaces, including the curve of the goggle lens.

Safety

The only thing in your line of sight is the film on the goggle lens, which is already there. The projector sits beside the inner corner of your eye, where your brow and nose already block the view. All optics near your face are plastic: an acrylic lens and an acrylic mirror inside a printed housing. The glass doublet and the glass mirror you have are for bench tests only.

Trade-off

The curved goggle lens works as a magnifying mirror, but used at an angle it smears the picture. A tilted lens cancels most of that in the middle of the picture. Toward the corners it gets softer, so keep HUD content away from the edges. Everysight solves this with custom freeform optics, which aren't available off the shelf.

Build order

  1. Order two plastic parts: an acrylic (PMMA) plano-convex or aspheric lens, Ø18 mm, f ≈ 30 mm, and a small piece of front-surface acrylic mirror (or a 15 mm acrylic right-angle prism).
  2. Film patch. Cut a 26 × 20 mm piece of the reflective film and stick it on the inside of the goggle lens where the build sheet says, flat side to the lens, no bubbles.
  3. Card mock-up of the projector: lens, mirror 6 mm behind it at 45°, panel above the mirror facing down. Wear the goggles and hold the mock-up beside the inner corner of your right eye, aimed at the patch, until the picture appears.
  4. Sharpen. Look at something far away. Slide the panel until the big digits are sharpest, then tilt the lens until small text sharpens. Repeat both once.
  5. Final housing: 3D-print it with a clip on the frame's top edge, like REKKIE's. The driver board sits against the inside of the frame between your eyes. The ESP32 and LiPo go in a pack on the strap, with a cable that runs under the frame foam.
